Folio Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Published by Folio Apps

Folio is a personal tracker for your investments and everyday money. It has no accounts, no servers of its own and no cloud. What you put in Folio stays on your device.

No sign-upNo adsNo analytics or trackingNo selling of dataCan’t move money

What Folio stores, and where

Your holdings, watchlists, alerts, accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, goals, settings, your optional name and profile picture, and your optional job and pay details are stored only on your device:

  • Windows: in Folio’s app data folder on your PC, with a backup copy and daily backups in the same place.
  • Android: in Folio’s private app storage. Folio’s data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

You can turn on encryption, which locks this data with a key tied to your Windows account (Windows) or held in the Android Keystore (Android). If you set a PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored.

What leaves your device

Folio contacts these services only to show you information. It never sends your balances, holdings amounts, transactions, budgets or personal details to them.

These services receive your device’s IP address as part of any internet request, and their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Linked banks

Bank linking is optional and read-only. You sign in to your bank on Plaid’s own secure page in your browser, so Folio never sees your bank username or password. Plaid can only read your balances and transactions for Folio; it can’t send, move or receive money. Folio uses Plaid keys that belong to you. The keys and access tokens are kept in a separate vault that is always encrypted on your device, never shown in the app and never included in backups or exports. Folio checks your bank only when you press Refresh.

When you unlink a bank, Folio asks Plaid to revoke its access. Plaid’s handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Alerts are checked on your device and shown as notifications by Windows or Android. You choose how much a notification shows, including hiding dollar amounts, in Settings, Privacy and security.

Files, backups and exports

Folio reads a file only when you choose it, for example a broker or bank export, a backup or a profile picture. Backups and exports are saved only where you choose to save them. A backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

Tester builds

Android tester builds are delivered through Firebase App Distribution, which Google operates. Google receives the email address you use to join the test and basic information about installs, under Firebase’s privacy terms. The Folio app itself contains no Firebase or Google analytics code.

Deleting your data

  • In Folio: Settings, Data and backups, Erase everything. This also unlinks any banks.
  • On Android, uninstalling Folio deletes its data.
  • On Windows, the Microsoft Store version’s data is removed when you uninstall it. The data folder of the standalone installer version stays until you delete it.

Because Folio’s data never reaches us, we can’t view, recover or delete it for you. Everything is in your control on your device.

Children

Folio is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. Folio itself collects nothing from anyone.

Not financial advice

Folio is a tracking tool. Prices may be delayed, and insights and trend ratings are general information calculated from your own data, not investment, tax, legal or financial advice.
